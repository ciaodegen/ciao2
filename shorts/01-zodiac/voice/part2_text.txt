He wore a black hood... with his symbol on the chest. September, nineteen sixty-nine. A young couple is having a picnic by a lake. He pulls a gun. Ties them up. Then he attacks. She doesn't make it. He survives, and describes the hood. On their car door, he leaves his symbol, and the dates of his attacks. Then, once again, he calls the police. Two weeks later. San Francisco. A man gets into a taxi. He kills the driver. Teenagers see it from a window, and call the police. Minutes later, police drive right past a man walking away. They don't stop him. Days later, a letter arrives, with a torn piece of the driver's shirt. Police release a sketch. Glasses. Short hair. Nobody is caught. Then he mailed a new code. Three hundred forty symbols. It would stay unsolved... for fifty-one years.
