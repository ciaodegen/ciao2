This code stayed unsolved for fifty-one years. The FBI tried. Codebreakers tried. Thousands of amateurs tried. Then, in twenty twenty, three amateur codebreakers cracked it. From the U.S., Australia and Belgium. The trick? The message was written diagonally, with a mistake inside. It said: "I hope you are having lots of fun in trying to catch me." But again... no name. Police had a main suspect, Arthur Leigh Allen. But his fingerprints and handwriting didn't match. He died in nineteen ninety-two. Never charged. In nineteen seventy-four, Zodiac claimed thirty-seven victims. Police can confirm five. And one code is still unsolved. Just thirteen symbols. It starts with: "My name is..." Fifty-seven years later, the case is still open. Maybe the answer... is right there.
