For fifty-seven years, nobody has caught him. They call him... the Zodiac. California, nineteen sixty-eight. A young couple is killed on a quiet road. Months later, he attacks again. This time, one victim survives. Then he calls the police and confesses, from a payphone just blocks away. Weeks later, three newspapers get letters. Each one holds a piece of a secret code. His demand: "Print it on the front page, or I kill again." Then another letter: "This is the Zodiac speaking." The name was born. Thousands tried to crack the code. But the ones who did it? A schoolteacher and his wife. In about a week. The message was chilling. But there was no name. And the last eighteen letters? Still a mystery. And for his next attack... he came wearing a costume.
